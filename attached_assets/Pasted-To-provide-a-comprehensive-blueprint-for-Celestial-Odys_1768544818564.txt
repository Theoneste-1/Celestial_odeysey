## **Part 1: The Master Technical Feature List**

This list outlines the "under-the-hood" requirements needed to build a 20-page-depth application.

### **1. Core Engine & 3D Rendering**

* **Rendering Pipeline:** High-fidelity PBR (Physically Based Rendering) for realistic material textures (e.g., the gas swirls of Jupiter, the metallic reflection of satellites).
* **Orrery Logic:** An astronomical coordinate system that calculates planetary positions based on **Julian Dates** (Real-Time or User-Defined).
* **Procedural Skybox:** A dynamic star-field that adjusts based on the camera’s "light pollution" settings, displaying nebulae and the Milky Way core.
* **Physics Engine:** Gravity simulations for moons and a "Time Scale" slider (, etc.).

### **2. Authentication & User Persistence**

* **OAuth 2.0 Integration:** Secure handshake protocols for **Google**, **GitHub**, and **Apple ID**.
* **State Management:** Utilizing Redux or Pinia to keep the user "logged in" across sessions and store their viewing preferences.
* **User Data Cloud:** A database (PostgreSQL/Firestore) to store:
* **Bookmarks:** Specific coordinates or planets saved for later.
* **Badges:** Progress markers for educational modules.
* **Theme Settings:** Choice between "Realistic" or "High Contrast" (for accessibility).



### **3. Interactive Navigation & Data**

* **Raycasting Selection:** Ability to tap on 3D objects to trigger an information overlay.
* **Contextual UI:** A 2D-HUD (Head-Up Display) that overlays the 3D scene, providing:
* **Orbital Data:** Speed, eccentricity, and inclination.
* **Atmospheric Composition:** Pie charts showing gas percentages.


* **Search & Autocomplete:** A global search bar with "fuzzy find" for all planets, moons, and major asteroids.

### **4. Media & Asset Management**

* **Asset Streaming:** "Lazy loading" 3D models to keep the initial app size small.
* **Audio Engine:** Procedural ambient "space" music and localized voiceovers for the educational facts.
